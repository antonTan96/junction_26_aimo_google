Middleware service
client registers ID, gets key
agent/client sends queries
Quota/price rules are created based on categories provided by the client.
If rules are violated, client is required to confirm, and possibly modify the rule.
If rule is not violated, middleware executes the order.

Example:
"I need 1,000 litres of lactose-free milk by Monday. Find an option that meets our requirements based on price and availability."

if the rule lactose-free milk exists and can hold 1000 more litres, the order is executed.
